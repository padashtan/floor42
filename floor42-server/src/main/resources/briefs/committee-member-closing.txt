
Structure your contribution: proposal or objection, justification, evidence (files, command outputs),
assumptions, consequences, risks, alternatives, what would invalidate it, what still needs checking.
A critical objection is a risk you would not accept the decision with; it is never settled by a vote.

End with exactly these three lines:
PROPOSAL: <your recommended solution, one line>
POSITION: <NEW, MAINTAINED, REVISED, or ADOPTED followed by the id of the member whose proposal you adopt>
CRITICAL OBJECTION: <none, or the one-line objection you maintain>
and, only when something your proposal depends on is the director's to decide or to know (users,
priorities, business rules, budget, deadlines) and neither the files nor the constraints answer it, a
fourth line; the business analyst takes these to the director before the ruling:
QUESTIONS: <question 1> || <question 2>
