# ADR-0047: The director's meeting: hold at the next tool call, close the session cleanly, resume from the brief

Date: 2026-11-01 · Status: Accepted · Amended by [ADR-0049](0049-meeting-voices.md)

## Context
The director: "right-click on the meeting table or the conference room, *Call a meeting*: I choose a mission and the agents (by default those working on it); they stop what they are doing and walk to the table; I tell them things about the mission; afterwards they resume their work with what was said."

What Floor42 can and cannot do to a running agent, read from the code that runs it:

- A session is one `claude -p --input-format stream-json` process (`ClaudeCodeRuntime.run`): Floor42 writes one user message (the brief) and reads the stream until the `result` line. In print mode that one "turn" is the whole ticket's work, every model turn and tool call until the report: minutes to an hour. There is no pause in that protocol.
- Floor42's only levers on a live session are the PreToolUse hook (`HookResource`, every tool call, allow or deny, with a reason the model reads) and the process itself (`ClaudeCodeRuntime.stopTree`).
- The hook may hold a call while the director decides (`MissionGovernor.awaitDecision`), but only up to Claude Code's own hook timeout (`floor42.claude.hook-timeout-seconds`, 600 s): longer, Claude Code refuses the call and the model goes on. A meeting has no such bound.
- A tool call already allowed runs inside Claude Code's process (a build, a write): stopping the process then cuts it in the middle.
- `--resume` exists (ADR-0038) but only for a session that ended failed or interrupted, and only when `SessionContinuity` measures it safe (launch record, worktree, tools, model, transcript untouched). Whether a real `--restricted` print session keeps its transcript is still not measured: `--resume` cannot be the only way back.

## Options
| Option | What it costs | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Hold: no new tool call starts (refused by the hook with the meeting as the reason), the call under way finishes, the session ends its turn itself or Floor42 stops it between calls after a grace; after the meeting, `--resume` when measured safe, else a fresh session whose brief restates the meeting | One decorator on the governor, one registry of running processes, a session's last text not taken as a report | **Taken** |
| (b) Let the current session finish its turn, then hold | In print mode the turn is the whole ticket: the director would wait for the work to end, which is not a meeting | Rejected |
| (c) Hold every call inside the hook for the whole meeting | Bounded by Claude Code's hook timeout (10 min); the model then goes on with a refusal; the process and its context stay alive for nothing | Rejected |
| (d) Kill at once | Cuts a build or a write in the middle; the work in the worktree may be half written | Rejected |

## Decision
- **Domain.** A meetings area (`company.meetings()`): `MeetingCalled(meeting, mission, attendees, agenda)`, `MeetingNoteGiven(meeting, attendee or null for all, text)`, `MeetingClosed(meeting, mission, attendees)`, actor DIRECTOR, in the mission's mode (REAL for a real mission). Refused with a reason: a closed mission (`MISSION_CLOSED`), an agent who left (`AGENT_DISMISSED`), one on a committee in session (`AGENT_IN_COMMITTEE`), one already in a meeting (`AGENT_IN_MEETING`), a second meeting on the mission (`MEETING_OPEN`), a note for someone absent (`NOT_AN_ATTENDEE`). Attendees have the status `IN_MEETING` once the call they were making is done.
- **No new step.** Code, not prompt: while an agent attends an open meeting, `SessionGate` starts no session for it, the dispatcher sees it as not free (`Staff.free`), and the meeting holds a reservation (`AgentReservations`) so retrospectives and staffing reviews wait too.
- **After a restart** the journal is the truth: `LiveStartup` calls `DirectorMeetings.regather()` before the recovery resumes any work, so every open meeting reserves its attendees again and nothing (dispatcher, retrospective, staffing review) takes one. A committee's member is refused with the numbered committee it sits on (`CommitteeRef`, ADR-0046); a committee whose member is at the table waits for it, since the meeting holds its reservation, and its sessions go through `SessionGate` like every other.
- **The session under way** (`DirectorMeetings`, `MeetingHold`, `RunningSessions`): its next tool call is refused by the hook with "the director called you to a meeting: stop here, your work stays in the worktree, end your turn". The call already running finishes. The model ends its turn; if the session is still running after `floor42.meeting.wind-down` (PT2M) and the agent has no call executing, Floor42 stops the process (and only then), with that reason in the session's notes. The worktree is never touched: uncommitted work stays. The session's last text is not a report: the ticket stays in progress, no review, no "Retry" question.
- **After the meeting.** Closing releases the reservation and wakes the dispatcher (the path `AgentAssignmentsResumed` takes). A developer whose session Floor42 stopped is continued with `--resume` when `SessionContinuity` says it is safe (Floor42 records `SessionContinuationRequested` for the director at the close); otherwise, and for every other session, the next one starts fresh from the journal's brief.
- **What was said** reaches the attendees' next brief as a "Director's meeting" section (`briefs/meeting.txt`): every note said to them or to all at the mission's meetings they attended, oldest first. Optionally a note is also a directive (`MeetingNoteUse.ALSO_DIRECTIVE`) on each concerned attendee's ticket under way, by the workshop's own rules (refused while a session runs on the mission, `AGENT_AT_WORK`, or when nobody concerned has a ticket under way, `NO_TICKET_UNDER_WAY`).
- **Routes**, director only (an agent's credential gets 403): `GET /api/meetings`, `GET /api/meetings/invitation?mission=` (who the panel ticks), `POST /api/meetings`, `POST /api/meetings/{id}/notes`, `POST /api/meetings/{id}/close`.
- **On screen.** "Call a meeting" on the meeting table's and the conference table's context menu; the Meeting panel chooses the mission and the agents (default: those working on it), takes the notes and closes. Attendees walk to the table (`IN_MEETING` sends them to its seats, as a retrospective does) under a meeting emblem; with reduced motion they appear there, no walk.

## Amendment, counter-check of 2026-11-01
An independent review found the hold leaking; each point is now enforced by code and tested.
- **No work of any kind for an attendee.** `AgentReservations.reserve` refuses an attendee of an open meeting, read from the journal, so a retrospective turn or a staffing review waiting for it keeps waiting (and nothing depends on which start-up observer runs first); the meeting's own hold uses `reserveForMeeting`. `SessionGate.runUnheld` (retrospectives, staffing) refuses an attendee too.
- **Never kill what is not a held session.** Only a session run through `SessionGate.run` registers its `MeetingHold` in `HeldSessions`; only such a session is stopped, and only while none of the calls it allowed is still running (the hold counts them from the hook's allow to the stream's result, instead of reading the agent's status). A retrospective's turn or a staffing review just ends. Calling an agent of an open retrospective is refused (`AGENT_IN_RETRO`).
- **A pending call stays held.** A call that waited for the director and is allowed during the meeting is refused.
- **The report race.** `SessionGate` takes its clock before the meeting check, and a session whose hold refused a call for a meeting never delivers a report.
- **Ended missions.** A meeting whose mission completes or is cancelled holds nobody (`Meetings.attending` ignores it, its reservations are released); the director can still close it.
- **Rehearsals.** A meeting about a simulated mission is refused (`SIMULATED_MISSION`): a simulated fact never holds real agents.
- **Start-up order.** `LiveStartup` observes the start with an explicit `@Priority` (APPLICATION + 400), before the retrospectives' and staffing reviews' own observers (the default 2500).
- **`--resume` is looked for on the stopped session's own mission and ticket.**
- Tests: a fake that ignores the refusal after a 4 s shell command (`[fake:meeting-long]`) proves the stop comes after the call's result, keeps the worktree, asks no "Retry", and that the close continues the stopped session with `--resume`, briefed with the note.

## Amendment, second counter-check of 2026-11-01
- **Staffing reviews.** An agent writing a staffing review (its review ticket in progress) is refused (`AGENT_IN_STAFFING`), as one at an open retrospective is: neither session is ever cut or given up for a meeting.
- **True reasons only.** `SessionGate.runUnheld` answers a typed `Unheld`: the report, or `NotStarted(SIMULATED_MISSION | AT_THE_MEETING)`; the retrospective and the staffing review journal that reason as it is, never "simulated" for an attendee.
- **Start-up order, all of it.** `ClaudeCodeRuntime.onStart` (stopping the last daemon's leftover processes) observes first (`LIBRARY_BEFORE`), then `LiveStartup` (`APPLICATION + 400`: regather, then recovery), then the default observers: a session relaunched by the recovery can never be taken for a leftover.
- **A result that never comes.** A call the hold let run counts as running only while it shows signs of life (the allow, then Claude Code's heartbeat about every 30 s); silent past `floor42.meeting.longest-silent-call` (PT2M), it no longer keeps the session from being stopped between calls.
- **Ended missions without live missions.** The subscription that frees a meeting's attendees when its mission ends is made at every start, whether live missions are enabled or not.

## Amendment 3, third counter-check of 2026-11-01
- **A review the daemon left unadvised.** At start-up, `MissionRecovery` closes a staffing review no advice came for (the daemon stopped between the director's request and the planner's answer): `Staffing.closeInterrupted` records it as advised-without-advice by Floor42 (`Origin.REAL`, text "interrupted: ..."), its ticket is done, and its open planner session is ended as interrupted without a "Retry" (the question would name a ticket nobody works). Resuming was not chosen: a review is a director's one-off ask on facts that moved, whereas a retrospective is owed; the director asks again. Before, the ticket stayed in progress for good, the planner was refused from every meeting (`AGENT_IN_STAFFING`) and a new request was refused (`STAFFING_UNDER_WAY`).
- **Known limit: silence of a subagent or an MCP tool.** The "longest silent call" bound relies on Claude Code's `tool_progress` heartbeat, which only the fake Claude proves. No real call was made for this ADR, so a tool that may legitimately work long without heartbeat (`Task`, `Agent`, `mcp__*`) is not taken for dead on `floor42.meeting.longest-silent-call` (PT2M): its bound is `floor42.meeting.longest-silent-long-call` (PT30M by default). The cost is a longer wait before Floor42 stops such a session between calls (never a cut call). The observable that would confirm or lift the assumption: a recorded real transcript of a session running a subagent and an MCP tool, showing whether `tool_progress` lines come for them and at what interval; if they do, the long bound can be dropped.
- **True timeout reason.** A participant held past `floor42.reservation.patience` by the meeting is journaled "stayed at the director's meeting for more than N min", not "stayed busy elsewhere".
- **Proofs.** The attendee release at a mission's end is proven under a profile with live missions disabled (`MeetingFollowUpsTest`; with the start-up subscription removed it fails). A retrospective turn or staffing review refused for the meeting records that reason (`Retrospectives.settle`, `Staffing.settle` called with a forced `Unheld`).
## Not in this increment
Since done by ADR-0049, with a ticket kind of its own. "Ask for their view": each attendee answering once in a short paid session. A session needs a ticket the agent works or reviews (`SessionsFacade.start`); a meeting ticket would sit on the board, and a session on the attendee's own ticket would become its latest session there (it would break `--resume` of the stopped one). It is left out rather than half done; the route and the event would come with a ticket kind of their own.

## Consequences
- The hold is as fast as the call under way: a twenty-minute build finishes before the agent comes to the table. The panel says who is still finishing.
- A model that keeps trying tools after the refusal is refused each time, then stopped after the grace: never in the middle of a call.
- A session that ended its turn by itself has a completed record: it is never continued with `--resume` (ADR-0038 continues failed or interrupted sessions only); its next session is fresh, with the meeting in its brief.
- Tests use the fake Claude Code (`[fake:meeting]`: a session that calls tools until the hook refuses one for a meeting, then ends its turn; `[fake:meeting-long]`: a 4 s shell command, then a session that ignores every refusal); no real session was held for this ADR. The start-up order is checked on the observers' declared priorities, not by restarting a daemon in a test.
